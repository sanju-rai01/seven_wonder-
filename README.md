The New Seven Wonders of the World
The New Seven Wonders of the World are seven remarkable monuments and
historical sites selected through a global poll organised by the
New7Wonders Foundation. The list was announced in 2007.
1. Great Wall of China --- China
The Great Wall of China is a huge network of walls and fortifications
built to protect Chinese states and empires from invasions. Construction
began more than 2,000 years ago and continued through several dynasties.
The wall stretches across mountains, deserts, and grasslands. It is
famous for its impressive length, watchtowers, and historical
importance.
2. Petra --- Jordan
Petra is an ancient city carved into rose-coloured rock cliffs in
southern Jordan. It was an important trading centre for the Nabataean
people. Its most famous building is Al-Khazneh, also known as the
Treasury, which has a beautifully decorated rock-cut façade. Petra is
admired for its architecture, water-management system, and dramatic
desert setting.
3. Christ the Redeemer --- Brazil
Christ the Redeemer is a large statue of Jesus Christ standing on Mount
Corcovado in Rio de Janeiro. With its arms stretched wide, the statue
overlooks the city and symbolises peace and welcome. Completed in 1931,
it is one of Brazil's best-known landmarks and is admired for its size
and location.
4. Machu Picchu --- Peru
Machu Picchu is an ancient Inca city located high in the Andes Mountains
of Peru. Built in the fifteenth century, it contains stone houses,
temples, terraces, and pathways fitted carefully into the mountain
landscape. Its exact purpose is still discussed by historians. The site
is famous for its remarkable stonework and beautiful mountain views.
5. Chichen Itza --- Mexico
Chichen Itza was a major city of the Maya civilisation on Mexico's
Yucatán Peninsula. Its best-known structure is El Castillo, also called
the Temple of Kukulcán, a stepped pyramid connected with Maya astronomy
and calendars. The site also includes a large ball court, temples, and
other ceremonial buildings.
6. Colosseum --- Italy
The Colosseum is a huge ancient amphitheatre in Rome. Built during the
Roman Empire and opened in the first century CE, it hosted public
spectacles, including gladiator contests. Its elliptical design, tiered
seating, and complex underground areas show the engineering skills of
the Romans. Today, it is one of Italy's most recognised historical
monuments.
7. Taj Mahal --- India
The Taj Mahal is a white-marble mausoleum in Agra, India, built by
Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
Construction began in 1632, and the complex is celebrated for its
balanced design, detailed marble decoration, gardens, and reflecting
pool. It is widely regarded as a masterpiece of Mughal architecture and
a symbol of love.
Conclusion
The New Seven Wonders of the World represent different civilisations,
cultures, and periods of history. Each site tells a unique story and
demonstrates human creativity, architecture, engineering, and artistic
achievement. They are important cultural treasures that people around
the world continue to admire and protect.

Note: This is the commonly used "New Seven Wonders of the World" list
announced in 2007. It is different from UNESCO's World Heritage List.
